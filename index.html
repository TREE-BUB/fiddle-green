Maine technology consulting

For organizations too small to be anyone else’s priority.

Fiddle Green works with small businesses, nonprofits, startups, and public agencies, and takes on the problems that quietly hold an organization back. That focus is deliberate. By not chasing big accounts, we can give smaller organizations the kind of attention they usually can’t get anywhere else.

{% include section-heading.html title="Who we're for" class="section-heading--sm" %}

Most of our clients have no one whose job is technology. The owner does it. Or the executive director, or the office manager, or a board member who’s good with computers. It gets handled in the margins of somebody’s real job, and it works until it doesn’t.

  • A restaurant whose website is costing it business, and no one on staff can fix it.
  • A nonprofit that keeps hearing it should be using AI, but has no idea where to start or whether it’s safe.
  • A community orchestra with an audio problem and a concert in ten days.
  • A founder with a good idea and no technical team.
  • A growing business that’s outgrown the software it set up three years ago, because nobody asked how the staff actually work before configuring it.
