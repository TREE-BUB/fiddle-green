Who We Help  /  Public Sector

A Maine technology partner for public sector work.

FIDDLE,GREEN supports municipal, county, state, and tribal entities with responsive technical support, staff training, and independent technology advising. Small enough to answer the phone; documented enough to satisfy procurement.

{% include section-heading.html title="Our approach" class="section-heading--sm section-heading--lede-tight" %}

We have deep experience on local, state, and federal contracts, and we know what that work asks of a vendor: documentation that survives an audit, timelines we can actually hold, and advice that stands up when someone else has to defend it.

Public sector technology is paid for by taxpayers and used by everyone, and we take that responsibility seriously. We also live here. The work we do for Maine towns, agencies, and tribal governments makes the places we call home a little better, and we are proud of that.
