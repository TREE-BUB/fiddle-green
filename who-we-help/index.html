We care about the human part of this work: sitting down with someone, understanding what is actually wrong, and solving it together rather than handing over a report. That does not change with the size of the client.

Whether you are one person asking for help on your own behalf, or a public agency running a contract that could change how an entire community gets served, you get the same attention and the same straight answers. The stakes change. The way we work does not.
