You can stop, change direction, or move on, and nothing is committed beyond the stage in front of you. mvp_steps: - num: "1" h: Frame the idea p: We define who it is for, the problem it solves, and the assumptions that would sink it if they are wrong. Those assumptions become the questions the prototypes are built to answer. - num: "2" h: Sketch it p: Paper screens, storyboards, and user flows. Sketches take minutes to change, so nobody is attached to the first draft. - num: "3" h: Build a clickable prototype p: An interactive wireframe, a designed mockup, a coded HTML prototype, or an AI-assisted simulation of a workflow, whichever answers your question most cheaply. - num: "4" h: Test with real users p: Guided simulations and usability sessions with the people who will use it. We watch where they hesitate or give up, and we simulate the missing system by hand where we have to. - num: "5" h: Engineer the MVP p: With the idea tested, we scope the smallest build that delivers the core value and build it, documented and in your accounts so you can own it or hand it on. method_title: Matching the method to the question method_note: We choose the cheapest prototype that can answer what you need to learn. method_steps: - h: Paper prototyping p: Sketch screens, layouts, or ideas by hand to test structure and flow. - h: Click-through mockups p: Simulate screen navigation and interactions, in a wireframing tool or in code. - h: Role-playing p: Script scenarios to explore how people use a product or service in context. - h: Storyboarding p: Draw a sequence of scenes to show the user journey or service experience. - h: Wizard of Oz p: Simulate a digital or AI-powered system by hand to test reactions before building it. - h: AI-assisted simulations p: Build an interactive workflow simulation from plain-language instructions in a fraction of the usual time. --- {% include steps.html title=page.mvp_title note=page.mvp_note steps=page.mvp_steps %} {% include callout.html eyebrow="Why prototype first" text="A prototype is a rough draft of your product that gets you feedback on your ideas and assumptions, before you pay to build the real thing." %} {% include steps.html title=page.method_title note=page.method_note steps=page.method_steps grid=true %}
