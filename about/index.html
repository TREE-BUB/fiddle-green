A fiddlehead is a fern before it unfurls. It is early, it is local, and it is growing on purpose — which felt like the right thing to be named after.

FIDDLE,GREEN is a Maine technology consulting company built for small organizations. We would rather help something get started well than be the people called in to fix it two years later, and we would rather leave you understanding your own systems than dependent on us for them.
