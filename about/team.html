Beyond the core team we keep a bench of trusted associates with deep expertise in grant management and workforce training, brought in when it is in the client's interest. Everyone on it was recruited from people we know and trust personally — never sourced cold.

We assign work to the best person for the job, not whoever happens to be free. Our people are paid fair market value, and creating real opportunity for them matters to us. It shows in the work.
