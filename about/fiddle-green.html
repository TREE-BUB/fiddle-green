FIDDLE,GREEN is a small technology company in Portland, Maine. We help individuals, startups, small businesses, nonprofits, and public agencies get their technology working — and understand it well enough to feel calm about it.

We started because too many people were paying for help they could not follow. Contracts written in acronyms, systems nobody could explain, and a quiet assumption that asking a question was embarrassing. We do the opposite: we sit down with you, sort out what is actually going on, write it down in words you can use, and leave everything in your name.

The work ranges from a home visit for a laptop that will not print to a multi-year assessment for a county office. What does not change is who shows up, how the price is set, or whether you get a straight answer.

{% include section-heading.html eyebrow="What we believe" class="section-heading--eyebrow" %}

Technology should not be a reason anyone in Maine feels left behind. We discount for nonprofits, keep a share of our hours open for work that could not otherwise happen, and never make someone feel foolish for asking.
