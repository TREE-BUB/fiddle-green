When the connection drops mid-shift, the instinct is to call the provider and
wait. Try these first — they resolve a good share of outages in under ten
minutes.

## Before you call anyone

Unplug the modem, wait thirty seconds, plug it back in, and give it a full two
minutes to come back. If there is a separate router, restart that next, not at
the same time. Check whether one device is affected or all of them: a single
laptop that cannot connect is a different problem entirely.

Then check the provider's outage map on your phone, using cell data rather than
the wifi. If the whole street is down, nothing you do in the building will help.

## What to have written down beforehand

The account number, the phone number for business support rather than
residential, and what your service is actually supposed to cost and deliver.
Keep it on paper, somewhere that does not require the internet to reach.
