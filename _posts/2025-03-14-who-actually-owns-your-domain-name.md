---
title: "Who actually owns your domain name?"
categories: ["Startups"]
summary: "If it is registered to a contractor's personal account, you do not own it. Here is how to check in five minutes."
---

Every few months we meet a company that cannot move its own website, because
the domain is sitting in an account belonging to someone who left in 2022.

It is worth five minutes to check. Look for three things:

- **The registrar account.** Whose email address can log in and transfer the
  domain? It should be an address the company controls, not a person's.
- **The registrant contact.** This is the legal owner of record. A contractor's
  name here is a problem even if you pay the renewal.
- **Auto-renew and expiry.** A lapsed domain can be bought by anyone, and
  getting it back is expensive when it is possible at all.

If any of those point at someone outside the company, fix it while the
relationship is good. Transfers are routine when everyone is on speaking terms
and miserable when they are not.
