Every engagement is quoted in writing before it starts, whichever shape it takes. Rates are sliding-scale, and public sector work follows the applicable schedule or solicitation.

We pride ourselves on working efficiently. The point is to solve the thing in front of you, not to run up hours — so tell us what you are dealing with on the first call, and we will tell you what it would run.
