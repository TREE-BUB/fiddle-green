Discounts do not stack. If more than one applies to you, you get the better of the two.

Closer to home

Incentives across Maine.

We do not bill travel time to clients elsewhere in Maine, and wherever we are working we set aside time for pro bono work in that community.

How we support Maine
{% include section-heading.html title="Pro bono and deep discounts" class="section-heading--sm section-heading--gap" %}

We hold back a limited number of pro bono and deeply discounted hours each quarter for work where we think we can make a real difference. If you have a project that would matter in your community and the cost is what is stopping it, send us a message and we will talk about it.

Tell us about the project
{% include section-heading.html title="How to ask" class="section-heading--sm section-heading--gap" %}

Just say so

On the first call, tell us the reduced rate is what you need. That is the whole process.

No application

Honor system

For the underrepresented-founder discount we do not ask for proof of who owns what. We take your word for it. Nonprofits need 501(c)(3) status, which is a matter of public record anyway.

Minimal paperwork

Same work, same people

A reduced rate does not buy you a junior version of us. It is the same work, scheduled the same way.

No difference
