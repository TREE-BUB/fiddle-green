Every engagement is quoted in writing before it starts, whichever shape it takes. Discounts for nonprofits and for underrepresented-led businesses are built into that number, and public sector work follows the applicable schedule or solicitation.

Many clients start with a package or a single project and move to a retainer once they know what they need. That is usually the cheapest arrangement for them, and the most useful one for us.

We pride ourselves on working efficiently. The point is to solve the thing in front of you, not to run up hours — so tell us what you are dealing with on the first call, and we will tell you what it would run.
