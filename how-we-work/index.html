Most firms treat the working relationship as whatever is left over once the technical work is done. We build it on purpose, because it is usually the part that decides whether the technical work was worth paying for.

That means a free first conversation before anyone commits, a written scope before anyone starts, one person who stays with your account instead of a queue, and notes you keep in language you can use. None of that is us being generous. It is what makes the work land, and it is why people call us back.
