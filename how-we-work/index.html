{% include section-heading.html title="How an engagement goes" class="section-heading--sm section-heading--gap" %}

One

A free call

Twenty minutes to hear the problem and say honestly whether we are the right people for it.

Two

A number in writing

An hourly range or a fixed project price, with the scope written in words you can check.

Three

The work

Done by the person you spoke to. If something changes the price, we stop and ask first.

Four

Notes you keep

Accounts, passwords, and changes documented plainly — in your name, under your control.
